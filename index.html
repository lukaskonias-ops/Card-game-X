Mission briefing, Captain.

You command the USS Enterprise. Three hostile forces are converging on Federation space: a Klingon D7 battle cruiser, a Romulan warbird with an intermittent cloak, and finally a Borg cube that adapts to your attacks.

Each turn you receive energy to play cards from your hand. Cards deal damage, raise shields, repair hull, and draw reinforcements. Shields absorb damage first and regenerate slowly — hull does not, unless your engineer works miracles.

Destroy all three enemies. The bridge is yours.
